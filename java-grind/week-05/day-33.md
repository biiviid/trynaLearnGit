# Day 33 — Material Design: Themes & Components
**Phase 3 · Week 5 · Friday**

---

## Goals
- Understand Material Design 3 as a design system, not just pretty colors
- Apply a theme and customize it
- Use core Material components: Toolbar, FAB, Snackbar, CardView

---

## Tasks
1. Add Material Design 3 dependency to `build.gradle` if not already present
2. Apply a Material theme, customize primary and secondary colors in `themes.xml`
3. Replace the default ActionBar with a `MaterialToolbar`
4. Add a FAB to your contact list that opens an "add contact" flow
5. Replace Toast messages with Snackbars — understand the UX difference between them

---

## Challenge
Wrap each contact item in a `MaterialCardView` with elevation and rounded corners. Long-pressing a card shows a `PopupMenu` with "Edit" and "Delete" options. How does this UX compare to swipe-to-delete?
