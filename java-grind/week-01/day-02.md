# Day 02 — Variables & Data Types
**Phase 1 · Week 1 · Tuesday**

---

## Goals
- Know the 8 primitive types and when you'd actually reach for each one
- Understand why `String` behaves differently from primitives
- Get comfortable with type casting and what information gets lost when you cast

---

## Tasks
1. Declare at least one variable of every primitive type — give them values that make sense for what they represent
2. Write a program that takes two integers and prints their sum, difference, product, and division result — pay attention to what happens with integer division
3. Try assigning a `double` to an `int` without casting. Read the error. Then fix it
4. Explore what happens when you concatenate different types with a `String` using `+`

---

## Challenge
You have a price of `19.99` and a quantity of `3`. Calculate the total, then calculate what the total would be after a `12%` tax. Print both values. Now think — which data type is "safest" for money, and why might `double` cause problems in real banking software?
